About MR Tex

Your trusted partner for innovative textile solutions with extensive industry experience

Our Story

Our Mission

At MR Tex, we take pride in our commitment to excellence. Our streamlined processes ensure that your specific requirements are met with precision. We offer access to technical files, physical samples, and a showcase of our product performance, ensuring transparency and quality assurance throughout the production process.

Our Vision

Our expertise meets innovation in the Ready-Made Garments (RMG) industry. Our seasoned team, with extensive experience gained from previous roles in renowned buying houses, is dedicated to providing a comprehensive range of high-quality RMG products.

Team Experience & Expertise

While our current collaborations continue to grow, our team's collective experience includes working with leading brands worldwide. This background strengthens our capability to deliver outstanding results.

Global Brand Experience

Our team has worked with renowned brands including Calvin Klein, Buffalo, Auchan, ETec, Splash, C&A, Primark, Reliance, M&S, Lidl, H&M, Umbro, Rolls Royce, Mustang, Katag, Zara, women'secret, B&C, Carrefour, Kappahl, Max, NewYorker, Walmart, USPA, and B&S.

Quality Assurance

We maintain the highest standards in our manufacturing processes. Our quality control systems ensure that every product meets international standards and exceeds customer expectations.

Efficient Logistics

Our efficient logistics from any location make us a reliable choice for timely deliveries worldwide. We understand the importance of meeting deadlines in the fast-paced textile industry.
